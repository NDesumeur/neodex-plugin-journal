# Journal

A personal diary, one page per day: write what you lived through, rate your mood, and find your old pages in a calendar coloured by your moods.

*Version française : [README.md](README.md)*

## Writing

- Today's page opens straight away: write in the large field ("How was your day?"). Everything is **saved automatically** as you type; the header shows the time of the last change.
- **Mood**: choose from 1 to 5, from "Very bad day" to "Great day". Clicking the same mood again clears it.

## Reading back

- The **calendar** shows the month, with weeks starting on Monday. Written days are marked in the **colour of your mood**; today is framed.
- **Click a day** to read or add to its page, even a past day you forgot.
- The arrows change month; **Today** returns to the present day.

## Statistics

Below the calendar: the number of days written this month, the total pages, and your **streak** of days written in a row (it counts up to yesterday if you have not written today yet).

## Data and sync

Your pages and moods are stored by NeoDex, backed up and synced between your PCs when sync is on. They are sent nowhere else.

## Install

In NeoDex: **NeoStore → Discover → Journal → Install**.

Building a plugin: see the [NeoDex SDK](https://github.com/NDesumeur/neodex-sdk).
